Title: Genomic Variant Interpretation in Rare Pediatric Neurological Disorders
Domain: Clinical Genetics / Rare Disease
Source Type: Research Seed Document

Background:
Whole exome/genome sequencing now achieves diagnostic yields of 25-40% in undiagnosed rare disease, but variant interpretation remains the bottleneck. Pediatric neurological disorders — epileptic encephalopathies, leukodystrophies, neuromuscular disorders — involve thousands of candidate genes. Computational tools for variant pathogenicity prediction, splicing impact, and genotype-phenotype correlation are essential for scaling clinical interpretation.

Key Research Questions:
1. Which VUS (variants of uncertain significance) in SCN1A, KCNQ2, STXBP1 can be reclassified using updated functional data?
2. How do deep learning splice predictors (SpliceAI, Pangolin) perform on intronic variants in leukodystrophy genes?
3. What gene panels have the highest diagnostic yield for infantile-onset epileptic encephalopathy?
4. Which protein domain hotspots (ion channel pores, kinase domains) concentrate pathogenic missense variants?

Target Genes and Conditions:
- SCN1A (Dravet syndrome): Nav1.1 sodium channel, >1800 known variants
- KCNQ2 (neonatal epilepsy): Kv7.2 potassium channel, gain/loss of function
- MECP2 (Rett syndrome): Transcriptional regulator, X-linked
- PMM2 (CDG-Ia): Phosphomannomutase 2, glycosylation disorder
- LMNA (Emery-Dreifuss MD): Lamin A/C, nuclear envelope

Relevant Databases and Tools:
- ClinVar (variant classifications, submitter concordance)
- gnomAD (population allele frequencies)
- OMIM (gene-disease relationships)
- SpliceAI (splice variant prediction)
- AlphaMissense (pathogenicity prediction)

Expected Output Types:
- genomic_variant: Variant records with gene, HGVS, classification, evidence level
- protein_interaction: Variant impact on protein complex assembly
- clinical_trial_record: Gene therapy trials for target conditions
