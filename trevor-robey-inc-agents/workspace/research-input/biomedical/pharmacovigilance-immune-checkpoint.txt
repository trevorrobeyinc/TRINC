Title: Pharmacovigilance Signals for Immune Checkpoint Inhibitors
Domain: Immuno-oncology / Drug Safety
Source Type: Research Seed Document

Background:
Immune checkpoint inhibitors (ICIs) — pembrolizumab, nivolumab, atezolizumab, ipilimumab — have transformed oncology but carry unique immune-related adverse events (irAEs). Post-marketing surveillance reveals emerging safety signals including myocarditis (0.1-1.1%), hepatitis, colitis, pneumonitis, and rare neurological toxicities. Combination ICI therapy amplifies these risks.

Key Research Questions:
1. What irAEs show disproportionate reporting in FAERS for anti-PD-1 vs anti-CTLA-4 agents?
2. Which biomarkers predict severe irAE risk (pre-existing autoantibodies, HLA subtypes, TMB)?
3. How do combination regimens (nivolumab + ipilimumab) alter the irAE spectrum vs monotherapy?
4. What is the time-to-onset distribution for cardiac irAEs across ICI classes?

Drugs Under Surveillance:
- Pembrolizumab (Keytruda): Anti-PD-1, broadest indication portfolio
- Nivolumab (Opdivo): Anti-PD-1, combination regimens
- Atezolizumab (Tecentriq): Anti-PD-L1, lung/bladder
- Ipilimumab (Yervey): Anti-CTLA-4, highest irAE rate as monotherapy
- Durvalumab (Imfinzi): Anti-PD-L1, stage III NSCLC

Signal Detection Sources:
- FDA FAERS (adverse event reports, PRR/ROR calculations)
- EudraVigilance (EU pharmacovigilance database)
- VigiBase (WHO global ICSR database)
- Published case series and meta-analyses (PubMed)
- CTCAE grading criteria for irAE severity classification

Expected Output Types:
- pharmacovigilance_signal: Disproportionality signals (PRR, ROR, IC) for ICI-irAE pairs
- clinical_trial_record: Active safety monitoring trials for ICI combinations
- molecular_properties: Biomarker profiles predictive of irAE susceptibility
