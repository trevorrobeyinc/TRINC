Citation-backed biomedical intelligence for enterprise decision-making

Autonomous multi-agent research engine that synthesizes pharmacovigilance signals, drug discovery data, and clinical trial landscapes from authoritative public databases. As of each delivery, every cited identifier is programmatically re-resolved against its issuing public registry (PubMed, ClinicalTrials.gov, Crossref/DOI, PubChem, RxNorm, ChEMBL, UniProt) and the result — VERIFIED, UNRESOLVED, or UNCHECKED — is recorded in a Reproducibility Manifest shipped with the report. Verification confirms each identifier resolves to a source record; it does not assess the source’s scientific conclusions.

Research Pipeline

How it works

01 — Scout

Systematic Database Scan

Autonomous scan of FDA registries, peer-reviewed literature, and pharmacological databases. Cross-reference normalization across identifier systems (PMID, NCT, RxNorm, PubChem CID, UniProt).

02 — Analyst

Quantitative Signal Analysis

Disproportionality metrics (ROR, PRR), drug interaction matrices, pharmacokinetic and pharmacodynamic profiling with molecular-level specificity.

03 — Synthesizer

Cross-Validated Synthesis

Signal hierarchy integrating scout and analyst outputs. Contradiction resolution, confidence tier assignment, and actionable clinical or strategic recommendations.

04 — Quality Audit

Citation Verification

Automated enforcement of citation density thresholds. Cross-reference validation, append-only evidence ledger with SHA-256 chain-hashed integrity, provenance tracking.

AWS Marketplace Delivery

Native AWS data-product delivery

01 — Amazon S3

Per-subscriber S3 buckets

Each AWS Marketplace subscription provisions a dedicated Amazon S3 bucket. Citation-traced research dossiers, citation-index CSVs, and provenance manifests land directly in the subscriber's bucket on a defined schedule.

02 — Amazon Athena

SQL-queryable citation indexes

Citation indexes ship as Parquet alongside the markdown report. Subscribers query the index directly with Amazon Athena — filter by PMID, NCT, ChEMBL CID, or DOI; join against internal pipeline data; build dashboards on top.

03 — Redshift / Snowflake

Federation to your warehouse

Output schemas are compatible with Amazon Redshift Data Sharing and Snowflake Secure Data Sharing. Federate dossier metadata into your existing analytics warehouse; enrich with your own RWE, claims, or trial data — no ETL pipeline to maintain.

04 — AWS Data Exchange

Listed on AWS Data Exchange

trevor-robey-inc data products are available through AWS Data Exchange for biotech investors, hedge funds, and pharma BD/licensing teams running portfolio diligence on AWS. Discoverable in the AWS Marketplace catalog; subscribed to with one click; billed through your existing AWS account.
