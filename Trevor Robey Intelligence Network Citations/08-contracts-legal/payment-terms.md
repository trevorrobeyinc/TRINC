## 2. Order first

2.1 **No work starts without a signed Order Form.** Provider begins work only after both parties have
signed the Order Form. If an AWS private offer is used for the order, Customer must also have accepted it
(Section 9).

2.2 **Purchase orders.** If Customer's process needs a purchase order, Customer sends the PO number before
work starts, and Provider puts it on the invoice. Terms printed on a purchase order, or accepted in a
supplier portal, do not add to or change the signed Order Form.

2.3 **Supplier setup.** On request, Provider supplies a Form W-9

## 3. Invoice

3.1 **When.** Provider issues one invoice for each Deliverable on its Delivery Date.

3.2 **What it shows.** Each invoice shows Provider's legal name and address; the invoice number and date;
the Order Form number and any PO number; the service and its Delivery Date; the fee; any tax itemized
under Section 6; the due date; and a reference to the payment instructions in the signed Order Form.

3.3 **Where it goes.** Provider emails the invoice from trevor@trevorobeyinc.com to the accounts-payable
contact named in the Order Form.

## 4. When payment is due

4.1 **Net 30 from the Delivery Date.** Payment is due within 30 calendar days after the Delivery Date
(operator decision 2026-11-04).

4.2 **No separate acceptance step.** Subject to Section 4.3, payment is not conditioned on a separate
acceptance step. If a Deliverable has a problem, the Refund Policy is how Customer raises it: a Material
Defect claim within 30 days of the Delivery Date, correction and redelivery within 15 Business Days, and a
refund of the full fee paid for that Deliverable if the correction fails.

4.3 **A pending claim and the due date.** A pending Material Defect claim does not pause the due date.
Payment stays due on Net 30, and if the correction fails, Provider refunds or credits under the Refund
Policy. The Order Form, section 6, says the same.

4.4 **Other terms.** A payment period other than Net 30 applies only if the Order Form states it and
Provider has signed it.

## 5. How to pay

5.1 **Currency and method.** Payment is in U.S. dollars, by ACH or wire transfer, to Provider's business
bank account in the name of Trevor Robey Intelligence Network Citations LLC.

5.3 **Other methods.** Other payment methods apply only if the Order Form states them.

## 6. Taxes

6.1 **Fees exclude tax.** Fees are exclusive of taxes. Customer pays the sales, use, value-added and similar
taxes that apply to the fees and that Provider itemizes on the invoice. Customer is not responsible for
Provider's income taxes.

6.2 **Exemptions.** If Customer claims an exemption, Customer provides a valid exemption or resale

## 7. Invoice disputes

If Customer disagrees in good faith with an amount on an invoice, Customer tells Provider in writing before
the due date, says what is disputed and why, and pays any undisputed amount on time. The parties then work
to resolve the dispute promptly. A claim that a Deliverable has a Material Defect follows the Refund Policy
and, under Section 4.3, does not pause the invoice's due date.

## 8. Late payment

8.1 **Interest.** Provider charges no interest on late payments.

8.2 **New orders.** While an undisputed invoice is overdue, Provider may decline to start work under a new
Order Form.

## 9. Purchases through AWS (only if an AWS private offer is used)

9.1 **Paid to AWS.** If an AWS Marketplace private offer is used for an order, Customer pays through AWS,
not to Provider. Sections 3, 4, 5 and 8 of these terms do not apply to that purchase.
Customer's payment terms are those of its AWS account.

9.2 **The invoice date is fixed.** AWS invoices Customer at 00:00 UTC on the invoice dates set in the
offer's payment schedule. That schedule is fixed when the offer is created and cannot be changed after
Customer accepts. Provider is paid after AWS receives Customer's payment. If an AWS private offer is ever
used for an order, its payment schedule has one invoice, dated to the scheduled delivery date in the Order
Form.

9.3 **Tax.** Tax on an AWS purchase is handled through AWS under Provider's AWS tax settings.

9.4 **Refunds.** Refunds on an AWS purchase follow Section 8 of the Refund Policy. AWS processes only
refunds that Provider authorizes.

9.5 **Availability.** Provider does not currently offer these services through an AWS private offer. Until it
does, this Section has no effect.

## 10. Refunds

Refunds and credits are governed only by the Refund Policy, which is Customer's sole and exclusive remedy
for Material Defects and Delivery Failures. A Deliverable that is never delivered is never invoiced on a
direct order.

## 11. Law

11.1 **Governing law.** California law governs these terms, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules
(operator decision 2026-11-04).

11.2 **Disputes.** Disputes go to the state and federal courts located in Sacramento County, California, the
forum stated in the agreement's key terms.

## 12. Nature of the services

Deliverables are research artifacts only. They are not medical, investment, legal or regulatory advice,
and they are not validated for clinical decision support. Provider processes no protected health
information (PHI), and Customer must not send any.
