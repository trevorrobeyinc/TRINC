## Questions for the future counsel hour

The first three are shared across the whole contract set (decided 2026-11-04). The rest are specific to this NDA.

1. **Governing law and venue.** California law, with exclusive jurisdiction in the courts of Sacramento County (Standard Terms §9; county chosen 2026-11-06): is that sound for this business, will out-of-state pharma and fund counterparties accept it, and should arbitration be added?
2. **Refund cap and sole remedy.** The fix-first rule (Material Defect reported within 30 days of delivery; corrected and redelivered within 15 business days; if still defective, refund capped at the fee paid for the affected deliverable, as the sole and exclusive remedy). It sits in the services paper, not this NDA, and is listed here so the hour covers the full set. It must stay consistent with the counsel-pending ADX draft `03-platforms/aws-data-exchange/refund-policy.md`.
3. **ChEMBL CC BY-SA share-alike.** Does share-alike carry over to a paid report that cites ChEMBL identifiers? Until counsel answers, ChEMBL identifiers are excluded from paid deliverables. The exclusion is stated where deliverable scope is defined (the SOW / order), because this NDA defines no deliverable scope.
4. **Purpose wording and hand-off.** The Purpose (revised 2026-11-04 after review) is limited to Party 2's evaluation of, and Party 1's performance *for Party 2* of, the two services, so Party 1 cannot use Party 2's information for other clients. Once an Order Form is signed, its section 9 makes PSA §11 govern confidentiality for that order and replace this MNDA for that information, because MNDA §2 limits use to the Purpose (which would cut across the Order Form's licence) and §5 lets the MNDA expire with its Term, while PSA §11 survives termination. Is that hand-off clean, including for information exchanged before the Order Form?
5. **PHI.** The LLC processes no PHI. Should "Changes to Standard Terms" say that neither party will disclose PHI under this MNDA? It is "None" for now, per the operator's instruction.
6. **Public records.** Standard Terms §3 already exclude information that "is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party". Is that enough for deliverables built from public registry records, or is an explicit carve-out wanted? (The old placeholder title proposed an "explicit public-domain carve-out"; adding one would be a Change to Standard Terms.)
7. **Signing authority.** With no operating agreement on file, is the signer's title (Managing Member, chosen 2026-11-06) enough to bind the LLC?

## Scope and posture (not part of the Cover Page)

- **No prices, turnarounds or payment terms here.** Prices come only from `OFFERINGS.md` (Trial Design Benchmark Pack $4,950; Failed Trial Archaeology $14,950). Payment terms (Net 30 after delivery) and the refund rule belong in the services paper.
- **Deliverable scope is defined in the SOW / order, not in this NDA.** That is where the ChEMBL exclusion (counsel question 3) is written.
- **Research artifacts only.** The services and data products are research artifacts. They are not medical, investment, legal or regulatory advice and are not validated for clinical decision support. No PHI is hosted, transmitted or processed.

## What the Standard Terms already say (operator summary; read the terms themselves before signing)

- §1: the MNDA is the signed Cover Page plus these Standard Terms. Per the Cover Page, the Cover Page controls if the two conflict.
- §3: four exceptions, including public information and information independently developed without using the other party's Confidential Information.
- §5: the MNDA starts on the Effective Date and ends at the end of the MNDA Term. Either party may end it on written notice, and confidentiality duties survive for the Term of Confidentiality.
- §8: Confidential Information is provided "as is".
- §9: the Governing Law applies and the Jurisdiction courts are exclusive. There is no jury waiver.
