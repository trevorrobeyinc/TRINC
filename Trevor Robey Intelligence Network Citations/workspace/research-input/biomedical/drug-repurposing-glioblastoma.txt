Title: Drug Repurposing Opportunities for Glioblastoma Multiforme
Domain: Neuro-oncology / Drug Discovery
Source Type: Research Seed Document

Background:
Glioblastoma multiforme (GBM) remains the most aggressive primary brain tumor with a median survival of 15 months despite standard-of-care treatment (temozolomide + radiation). The blood-brain barrier limits therapeutic options, making drug repurposing an attractive strategy to identify existing compounds with CNS penetration and anti-tumor activity.

Key Research Questions:
1. Which FDA-approved drugs show in-vitro activity against GBM cell lines (U87, T98G, U251)?
2. What molecular targets (EGFR, PDGFRA, IDH1, MGMT, TERT) are druggable with existing compounds?
3. Which repurposed drugs have progressed to clinical trials for GBM (ClinicalTrials.gov)?
4. What ADMET properties predict BBB penetration for candidate compounds?

Notable Candidates Under Investigation:
- Chloroquine/Hydroxychloroquine: Autophagy inhibition, multiple Phase II trials
- Metformin: AMPK activation, anti-proliferative effects, NCT02780024
- Disulfiram: ALDH inhibition + copper chelation, NCT03363659
- Mebendazole: Tubulin binding, preclinical GBM models show IC50 < 1μM
- Valproic acid: HDAC inhibition, radiosensitization, NCT00302159

Relevant Databases:
- DrugBank (compound profiles, targets, ADMET)
- ChEMBL (bioactivity data, IC50/EC50 values)
- ClinicalTrials.gov (active GBM trials with repurposed drugs)
- PubChem (molecular properties, bioassay data)
- COSMIC (somatic mutation profiles in GBM)

Expected Output Types:
- molecular_properties: Compound profiles with SMILES, MW, LogP, PSA, BBB permeability
- clinical_trial_record: Active/completed trials for repurposed drugs in GBM
- pharmacovigilance_signal: Safety signals for candidate drugs in CNS context
