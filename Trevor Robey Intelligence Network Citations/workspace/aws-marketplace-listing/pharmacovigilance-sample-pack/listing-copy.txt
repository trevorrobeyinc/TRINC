## Long description (Markdown, AWS Data Exchange generic-template structure)

### Product Summary

This free sample pack from trevor-robey-inc demonstrates the citation-traced pharmacovigilance methodology used in our paid Investor Pre-Mortem and Failed Trial Archaeology dossiers. Every adverse-event signal in the included CSV files maps to a verifiable identifier (PMID, NCT, ChEMBL CID, RxNorm, PubChem CID, UniProt, or DOI) in a public source database.

The sample focuses on immune checkpoint inhibitor (ICI) class adverse events — myocarditis, colitis, pneumonitis, hepatitis — plus three glioblastoma drug-repurposing safety signals derived from FDA AEMS disproportionality analysis and peer-reviewed literature.

### Key Features

- **10 pharmacovigilance signals** with AEMS Reporting Odds Ratios (ROR) or Proportional Reporting Ratios (PRR), severity tier classification, and recommended clinical actions
- **29-row citation index** linking every signal back to a verifiable public-database identifier
- **No PHI** - all data sourced from FDA AEMS, DailyMed, RxNorm, PubMed, ClinicalTrials.gov, ChEMBL, UniProt
- **Refund-backed citation-audit SLA** on all paid trevor-robey-inc deliverables (free sample carries no SLA)
- **AWS-native delivery** for paid products: Amazon S3 buckets, Athena-queryable Parquet, Redshift and Snowflake federation

### Use Cases

- **Biotech investors and hedge funds** evaluating ICI-class assets for portfolio risk
- **Pharma BD/licensing teams** running competitive due diligence on immuno-oncology candidates
- **Pharma R&D competitive intelligence** benchmarking trial-design safety patterns
- **Patient advocacy groups** preparing plain-language safety communications

### Data Sources

| Source | Use |
