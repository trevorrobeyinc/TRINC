# Trevor Robey, INC. — AWS Data Exchange Subscriber Support Process

_Satisfies the ADX provider criterion: a defined customer-support process and support
organization, with subscriber inquiries answered within one business day._

- **Support contact:** trevor@trevorobeyinc.com (monitored business mailbox); subscribers
  may also reach us via the AWS Marketplace / AWS Data Exchange messaging channel.
- **Hours of operation:** Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM–5:00 PM U.S. Central Time, excluding U.S.
  federal holidays.
- **Response SLA:** We acknowledge every subscriber support request within **one (1)
  business day**, meeting AWS Data Exchange's subscriber-response requirement.
- **Resolution targets by type:**
  - *Access / delivery* (cannot retrieve a data set or revision): triage ≤ 1 business day,
    resolution target 2 business days.
  - *Data / citation questions* (provenance, methodology, a specific PMID/NCT/DOI):
    substantive answer ≤ 2 business days.
  - *Suspected data error:* investigate ≤ 1 business day; if confirmed, publish a corrected
    revision and notify affected subscribers.
- **Escalation:** Unresolved issues escalate to the principal (Trevor Robey, owner) for
  direct handling; subscribers may request escalation at any time.
- **Billing / subscription:** handled through AWS Marketplace; we assist on provider-side items.
- **Ownership:** Support is owned by the principal, with per-product runbooks so coverage is
  consistent.
