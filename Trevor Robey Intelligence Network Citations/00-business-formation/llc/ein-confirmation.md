>
> **Where the EIN does — and does not — come from (verified 2026-07-22).** The EIN is a **federal
> IRS** identifier. It is **NOT** issued by, stored in, or retrievable from **bizfileOnline / the
> California Secretary of State**. Verified against both CA artifacts on file: the
> [filing receipt](./receipt-articles-of-organization-2026-05-06.pdf) (Receipt No. 13704001;
> Document No. B20260217250) and the certified copy (Certificate No. 457169837) — **neither contains
> an EIN**. Neither CA form collects one: Articles of Organization (LLC-1) captures name, address,
> agent for service of process, and management structure; the Statement of Information (LLC-12)
> doesn't ask either. So no California filing will ever produce an EIN.
>
> **How to get it:** apply free + instantly online at
> <https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online>
> (Form SS-4 equivalent). The confirmation arrives as **IRS Notice CP 575** — save that PDF and
> record its details here. If CP 575 is ever lost, request a **Letter 147C** from the IRS instead.
>
> **What it unblocks:** (1) business bank account (Mercury/Relay), (2) the **AWS Marketplace "Legal
> business name"** update — AWS wants stamped Articles **+ EIN** via a Support case, which is why
> that field still reads the wordmark "Trevor Robey Inc"; (3) Global KYC business verification, if
> it is ever needed (currently ruled out — see `03-platforms/business-identity-map.md`).
> It does **NOT** gate AWS disbursement — that was completed 2026-07-22 on the existing US ACH
> account, without an EIN.
>
> **Application rules (irs.gov page, updated 2026-10-16, read 2026-10-21):** one sitting, no saving;
> the session expires after 15 minutes idle; one EIN per responsible party per day; hours Mon–Fri
> 6:00 a.m.–1:00 a.m. ET, Sat 6:00 a.m.–9:00 p.m. ET, Sun 6:00 p.m.–midnight ET; an approved EIN is
> issued immediately online with a confirmation letter to print. Every answer, pre-filled:
> [`../compliance/filing-walkthrough-2026-10.md`](../compliance/filing-walkthrough-2026-10.md) § 1.
