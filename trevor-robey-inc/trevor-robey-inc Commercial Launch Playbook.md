## Section 2 — Platform launch sequence

Two of the operator's six "distribution platforms" are misclassified. **Outlier.ai (Scale AI) and Mindrift.ai (Toloka) are RLHF / expert-labeling labor markets**, not data marketplaces. They pay Trevor's *time* at $30–$118/hr to label AI-training data; they cannot host an OpenClaw research-pack listing. Treat them as a side-cash channel only — never a product channel. **Kaggle has no commerce layer for datasets** — useful only as a free SEO/credibility breadcrumb that points to a paid listing elsewhere.

The defensible top three are **Datarade, AWS Data Exchange, and HuggingFace Hub (gated)**, in that order.

### #1 Datarade — fastest revenue path for citation-backed research packs

Datarade is the only platform of the six purpose-built for selling **report-style data products at $2,500–$120K+ price points**. Listing is free on the Basic Subscription; KYC is days; fulfillment happens off-platform so Trevor invoices buyers directly and keeps 100% of revenue on Basic. The SEO storefront drives inbound enterprise leads that fit the Custom and Enterprise tiers naturally — buyers at $15K+ want to talk to a human anyway.

- **Onboarding URL:** https://datarade.ai/company/contact/data-providers (live; the legacy `/become-a-data-vendor` path returned 403 to fetcher but search snippets confirmed the intake form).
- **File formats:** Format-agnostic at listing level; PDF reports are explicitly supported. Sister platform Monda (https://monda.ai) handles CSV/JSON/XML/API delivery if needed later.
- **Metadata schema:** Storefront + product pages requiring data category (560+ taxonomies), use cases, geographic coverage, pricing model, delivery method, sample availability, GDPR/CCPA flags, update frequency.
- **Vendor verification:** KYC of legal entity (single-member LLC works); media check; "Verified data provider" badge available on Professional tier `[inferred]`.
- **Payouts:** Off-platform — vendor invoices buyer directly. Datarade collects commission on closed deals.
- **Revenue share:** Basic subscription is free for vendor listing. The Monda commerce tier shows **30% commission on Standard / 20% on Professional** of closed deal value (https://monda.ai/pricing). Standalone Datarade marketplace listing without Monda may differ — confirm on the intake call.
- **Time-to-first-listing:** **3–10 days** `[inferred]` from KYC fast-track + free provisioning.
- **Time-to-first-revenue:** **30–120 days** `[inferred]` based on B2B inbound-lead → close cycles. **No published solo-operator case study with dated dollar figures was located** — Datarade testimonials are qualitative ("enables us to publish our datasets in several channels"). Validate by direct outreach to Datarade's vendor success team.

### #2 AWS Data Exchange — highest-trust enterprise channel, but slow

Pharma buyers (Moderna, Takeda, Novo Nordisk, Vyaire) are confirmed active subscribers on the platform. The 3% public listing fee is the lowest revenue share among commerce-enabled options, with private offers tiered down to 1.5% for $10M+ contracts — ideal for OpenClaw's Enterprise tier. PDFs, CSV, Parquet, and APIs are all supported natively. The reason this is #2 not #1 is the 2–6 week qualification gate plus 60–180 day enterprise procurement cycles for first revenue.

- **Onboarding URL:** https://docs.aws.amazon.com/data-exchange/latest/userguide/provider-getting-started.html → register at https://aws.amazon.com/marketplace/management/ → file an AWS Support case requesting Data Exchange qualification.
- **File formats:** S3-hosted assets — CSV, Parquet, JSON, PDF, images; Redshift datashares; Lake Formation datasets (preview); APIs via API Gateway.
- **Metadata schema:** Product details, logo, support contact, Data Subscription Agreement (DSA), categories, regions, update frequency, dataset/revision/asset hierarchy, refund policy.
- **Vendor verification:** Multi-step KYC including AWS account in good standing, business entity in eligible jurisdiction, public seller profile, tax interview (W-9/W-8), bank account, and a separate Data Exchange qualification reviewed manually by AWS Marketplace Seller Operations. Sensitive data routes through the Extended Provider Program with additional review.
- **Payouts:** **Monthly disbursement** to registered bank account on a rolling cadence based on account anniversary — funds disbursed only after AWS collects from subscriber, so net effective ~30–60 days from invoice.
- **Revenue share / fees:** **3% on public listings** post-Jan 2024 fee restructure; **2% for $1M–$10M private offers, 1.5% over $10M, 1.5% on renewals**, +0.5% for CPPO. S3 storage ~$0.023/GB/mo separate.
- **Time-to-first-listing:** **2–6 weeks** (Clazar's published guide states 1–4 weeks for AWS Marketplace registration if information is accurate, plus the additional Data Exchange qualification review).
- **Time-to-first-revenue:** **60–180 days** `[inferred]`. Public seller success stories (Acoer, IQVIA, Verisk, S&P Global, Foursquare) are heavily enterprise — **no solo-operator biomedical case study with revenue + timeline located**. AWS publishes healthcare *buyer* case studies (Moderna, Takeda) but not solo-seller ones.

### #3 HuggingFace Hub gated dataset — strategic top-of-funnel, not direct revenue

HF Hub does not have a native paid-dataset commerce layer. There is no Stripe checkout for datasets; "paid biomedical dataset" examples (argilla/medical-domain, etc.) are open and free with revenue happening via the parent company's separate enterprise SaaS. What HF *does* offer is the **gated dataset feature** (https://huggingface.co/docs/hub/datasets-gated), which lets the operator capture lead intent — Company name, country, intended use — before granting access. Convert qualified leads to paid sales off-platform; HF takes 0%.

- **Onboarding URL:** https://huggingface.co/join → create dataset repo → enable gating in repo settings.
- **File formats:** Parquet (preferred for the dataset viewer), CSV, JSON/JSONL, Arrow, audio, images, WebDataset. PDFs uploadable but not viewer-rendered. Per-file 50GB practical limit.
- **Metadata schema:** YAML front-matter in README.md (license, language, task_categories, tags, source_datasets) plus optional `extra_gated_fields` (Company, Country, intended use, license acknowledgment); `extra_gated_eu_disallowed: true` available.
- **Vendor verification:** None platform-side — author manually approves access requests via UI or API.
- **Payouts:** None — HF does not handle commerce. Operator collects payment off-platform.
- **Revenue share / fees:** 0% from HF on any off-platform sale. Storage above 1TB is $18/TB/mo on Pay-as-you-go; optional $9 PRO subscription.
- **Time-to-first-listing:** **<1 day** (free signup → upload → gate).
- **Time-to-first-revenue:** **Indeterminate** — depends entirely on outbound conversion of access-requesters. **No published solo-operator HF gated-dataset revenue case study was located.**

OFAC/BIS screen on the legal entity AND ultimate beneficial owner via SAM.gov consolidated screening (https://sanctionssearch.ofac.treas.gov) — 5 minutes, one-shot. ## Section 6 — Pre-publication due-diligence checklist (5 YES/NO items, <30 min each)

Before any pack ships to Datarade, AWS Data Exchange, or HuggingFace gated, all five must be YES.

**1. Citation reproducibility — do all DOIs/PMIDs in the pack resolve to live primary sources?** Tool: in-house Python validator using DOI HEAD checks at https://doi.org, NCBI E-utilities `efetch` for PMIDs (https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK25501/), Crossref metadata-match (https://api.crossref.org), Unpaywall OA status (https://api.unpaywall.org). Pass condition: 100% of DOIs return 200/302 to publisher; 100% of PMIDs return valid `<PubmedArticle>`; metadata fields match within Levenshtein distance <5. Time: ~5 min for typical pack <200 citations.

**2. License compliance — are all source databases cleared for the intended commercial use?** ✅ FAERS/AEMS public; ✅ ClinicalTrials.gov public; ✅ PubChem public; ⚠️ UniProt CC BY 4.0 attribution present in appendix; 🚩 ChEMBL CC BY-SA 3.0 — confirm whether outputs are a "derivative work" obligated to share-alike (https://chembl.gitbook.io/chembl-interface-documentation/about, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/); 🚩 **DrugBank — STOP-SHIP unless commercial license signed** (https://go.drugbank.com/legal/terms_of_use). Pre-flight tool: a static License Register Markdown file in the pack repo, plus a regex gate scanning the deliverable for DrugBank IDs (`DB\d{5}`); if matched and no commercial license active, fail the build. Time: ~10 min re-verification, <1 min for the regex gate.

**3. PHI/PII screening — no patient identifiers in the pack.** Recommended tool for solo Mac operator: **Microsoft Presidio** (https://github.com/microsoft/presidio) — free, open-source, runs locally so no PHI ever leaves the laptop; out-of-the-box recognizers cover the 18 HIPAA Safe Harbor PHI types (https://github.com/microsoft/presidio-research/blob/master/docs/requirements/industry/hipaa/readme.md). AWS Comprehend Medical (https://aws.amazon.com/comprehend/medical/) and Google Cloud Healthcare API DLP (https://cloud.google.com/healthcare-api) are HIPAA-eligible but require sending text to cloud and a BAA — overkill at solo scale. Pass condition: zero high-confidence Presidio matches (score >0.6) other than intentionally allowed entity types (cited paper authors). Time: ~3 min per pack.

**4. Freshness / staleness windows — is every cited source within max age?**

| Source | Actual 2026 cadence | Max staleness rule |
|---|---|---|
| FAERS / AEMS | Daily/real-time since Aug 22, 2025; FAERS→AEMS migration completed Mar 11, 2026 (https://fis.fda.gov/extensions/FPD-QDE-FAERS/FPD-QDE-FAERS.html still posts quarterly extracts) | ≤30 days for safety signals; ≤90 days for trend analyses |
| ClinicalTrials.gov | Real-time (responsible parties update continuously) | ≤14 days for trial-status claims |
| ChEMBL | ~6–9 month major release cycle (ChEMBL 35 Dec 2024 → ChEMBL 36 Sept 2025 — https://chembl.gitbook.io/chembl-interface-documentation/downloads) | Latest release; flag pack version explicitly; refresh within 60 days of new release |
| PubMed | Daily | ≤7 days since last sync |
| DrugBank | Quarterly major releases `[inferred]` (https://go.drugbank.com/releases/latest) | ≤90 days AND only if commercial license active |
| UniProt | ~8-week cadence (~6/yr) `[inferred]` | ≤60 days |

Pack header must state `Data sources last refreshed: YYYY-MM-DD` and each row must be ≤ its threshold. Tool: small `freshness.yaml` + 10-line check script. Time: ~3 min.

**5. Hallucination / citation-correctness audit (operator's choice).** Two-layer audit. **Layer 1:** LLM-as-judge cross-validation — for every citation-bearing claim, a separate model (different vendor than the pack-authoring model) verifies (a) source exists, (b) source supports the specific claim, (c) directionality matches. Output per-claim `{verified, evidence_quote, confidence}`. **Layer 2:** manual 10% random spot-check by Trevor — pull 10% of citations, open source PDF, confirm. Tooling: **Ragas** (https://docs.ragas.io) for RAG-evaluation `faithfulness`, `answer_relevance`, `context_precision` metrics — best fit for citation-grounded packs; **TruLens** (https://www.trulens.org) for ongoing monitoring dashboards if scaling; **DeepEval** as lighter-weight alternative. Pass rule: Layer 1 ≥95% verified at confidence ≥0.7; Layer 2 = 100% of manual sample pass; any Layer 1 failures corrected before ship. Time: Layer 1 ~15 min runtime; Layer 2 ~10–15 min.

## Section 7 — Supplementary research: monetization models, buyer profiling, and distribution networks for independent B2B research assets

*Appended 2026-04-26. Sources: secondary research bundle covering MIT data-monetization frameworks, Bain/NewtonX B2B buyer-group studies, Ipsos panel-blending guidance, ESOMAR AI-quality standards, and platform-level commission/onboarding data.*

### 7.1 Architecture of data monetization and the intelligence economy

The global data marketplace platform sector was valued at ~$1.49 billion in 2024 with projections reaching $5.73 billion by 2030 (CAGR 25.2%); alternative econometric models forecast $8.38 billion by 2033 (CAGR 21.44%). Large enterprises allocate ~$80M/yr (≈2% of revenue) to data budgets. MIT research identifies four data-monetization strategies — internal optimization, wrapping (product enhancement), and direct selling — and for independent analysts/boutique intelligence firms operating dedicated domains, **direct selling is the primary value-capture mechanism**.

Bain & NewtonX research finds brand familiarity functions as "decision-insurance" in B2B procurement: ~81% of B2B purchases go to vendors the buying group already knew on day one; only 4% go to vendors known solely by specialized internal experts. Buyer groups pay 3:1 more for known vendors. Consequently, independent publishers must prioritize brand building, authoritative distribution, and meticulous product structuring.

### 7.2 Buyer-cohort psychographics

**Private equity / institutional buy-side.** Operates on the "Four P's" due-diligence framework spanning commercial, financial, tax/regulatory/legal, and integrity/ESG diligence. Independent researchers can structure deliverables to explicitly answer the questions posed during these evaluation periods. Key opportunity: third-party verification of market conditions to detect anomalies in seller-disclosed data (the "grey zone" between legal disclosure and optimistic framing).

**Startup founders.** Suffer from market-validation uncertainty, credibility challenges, and limited strategic bandwidth. Pre-packaged citation-traced asset dossiers give them independent facts to support fundraising; without external data, founders operate on intuition, leading to misplaced capital and missed opportunities.

**Marketing agencies & consultancies.** Procure external data to inform strategic advice and digital campaigns. The labor-intensive processes of data collection, cleaning, and verification represent operational bottlenecks they prefer to outsource. Access to verified B2B contact data and real-time search trends drives higher conversion rates and superior client retention.
