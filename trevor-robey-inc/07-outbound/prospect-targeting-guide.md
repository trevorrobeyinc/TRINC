## 2. Ideal Customer Profile

### High-Priority Titles

| Title | Persona | Typical Budget Authority |
|-------|---------|------------------------|
| Director, Competitive Intelligence | CI Director | $50K-$500K annual for external data/intelligence |
| VP, Pharmacovigilance | CI Director | $100K-$1M+ for safety monitoring tools |
| Head of Alternative Data | Quant Analyst | $200K-$2M+ for data sourcing |
| Director, Target Discovery | Discovery Scientist | $100K-$500K for external validation data |
| Chief Data Officer | Executive Sponsor | $500K-$5M+ for data strategy |

### Company Signals (Positive)

- **Patent cliff exposure** — Compounds going off-patent 2026-2030 create urgent need for competitive intelligence and pipeline replenishment data
- **Active Phase III trials** — Companies with late-stage assets have pharmacovigilance obligations and competitive monitoring needs
- **Recent M&A activity** — Acquiring companies need rapid intelligence on acquired pipelines and competitive positioning
- **Public AI/data strategy** — Companies publicly investing in data-driven decision making are actively evaluating new sources
- **Regulatory actions** — Companies receiving FDA complete response letters or safety warnings need pharmacovigilance data immediately
- **Hiring CI/data roles** — See Job Board Signals above

### Disqualifiers

- **<50 employees with no research budget** — Small organizations without research procurement budget. Exception: academic labs and boutique consultancies are standard-tier targets ($2,500-$4,950 packs).
- **Academic institutions (for enterprise tier)** — Different procurement cycle, typically grant-funded. However, PIs and research directors are strong standard-tier buyers — shorter sales cycle, often purchase same-day with grant funds. Treat as volume plays.
- **Existing large CI contracts** — If they already have GlobalData/Evaluate/Citeline enterprise contracts, this becomes a displacement sale (harder, longer cycle). Deprioritize but don't exclude — position as supplementary/specialized
- **Government agencies** — Complex procurement, long cycles, low price tolerance

## 4. Discovery Call Script

### Opening (2 minutes)

Reference the specific company signal that triggered outreach:

> "I reached out because I noticed [Company] has [specific signal — e.g., three compounds entering Phase III this year / a recent FDA complete response letter / posted a Director of CI role last month]. That usually means your team is under pressure to [synthesize competitive data faster / strengthen pharmacovigilance monitoring / build out data capabilities]."

### Discovery Questions (10 minutes)

1. **Current data sourcing**: "How does your team currently source pharmacovigilance or competitive intelligence data? What tools or vendors are you using?"
2. **Friction points**: "What's the biggest bottleneck in going from a research question to an actionable answer? Is it sourcing, synthesis, or getting it in front of decision-makers?"
3. **AI/hallucination concerns**: "Has your team experimented with LLM-based research tools? What was the experience with citation accuracy?"
4. **Compliance needs**: "For your use case, how important is it that every data point traces back to a primary source — a PMID, NCT number, or regulatory filing?"
5. **Data infrastructure**: "If you were to receive structured intelligence data, where would it need to land? Snowflake? Internal data lake? Email is fine too?"
6. **Budget and timing**: "Is this something your team is actively evaluating solutions for this quarter, or more of a Q3/Q4 priority?"

### Live Demo (5 minutes)

Screen share the sample deliverable:

1. Open `pharmacovigilance-sample-deliverable.md` — walk through methodology, executive summary, signal deep-dive
2. Click a PMID (30232222) live — open pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30232222 in browser to demonstrate it resolves
3. Show citation index CSV — highlight the 7 identifier types and 41 verified citations
4. Show schema definition — explain Snowflake/BigQuery delivery format

> "Every identifier in this report is independently verifiable. You can click any PMID, NCT number, or RxNorm ID and it resolves in its source database. This is the trust mechanism — no hallucinated references, no fabricated data."

### Objection Handling

**"We already use GlobalData / Evaluate / Citeline."**

> "Those are excellent platforms for broad coverage. Where we complement them is depth on specific research questions — when you need 200+ citations synthesized into a structured output on a single topic in 48 hours, not a dashboard you browse. Think of us as the special ops team your CI platform calls when it needs deep intelligence on a specific compound or safety signal."

**"How is this different from ChatGPT / Perplexity?"**

> "General-purpose LLMs hallucinate citations at a rate of 30-70%. Every dossier we deliver passes automated citation verification as a build-time gate — if an identifier doesn't resolve in its source database, it's excluded. Our outputs carry 200+ verified citations with a cryptographic provenance chain and a refund-backed citation-audit SLA. We deliver structured due-diligence dossiers, not chat responses."

**"The price is high for data."**

> "A single pharmacovigilance signal miss can cost $50M+ in regulatory action, trial delays, or market withdrawal. Our reports synthesize what would take a team of analysts 2-3 weeks into a 48-hour deliverable with full citation backing. The $15K investment pays for itself if it catches one signal your current tools miss."

**"Can we do a pilot first?"**

> "Absolutely. Let's run a focused Investor Pre-Mortem on an asset relevant to your current pipeline. You choose the compound, mechanism, or trial readout; we deliver the dossier in 5–10 business days; you verify every citation independently. $14,950 for the standard tier ($9,950 anchor or $19,950 custom with KOL primary). If the quality meets your bar, we discuss recurring engagements. If it doesn't, you've spent less than a week of a single analyst's time."

### Close (2 minutes)

> "Based on what you've described, I think the best next step is a focused pilot mission on [topic they mentioned]. We'll produce a full deliverable — 40+ pages, 200+ verified citations, structured data in whatever format your team needs. You verify the citations, evaluate the quality, and we go from there. Can we get that started this week?"
